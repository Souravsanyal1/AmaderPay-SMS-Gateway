Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 3, 2026

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using NexoraPay services.

1. Introduction

Welcome to NexoraPay ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal and operational data of our merchants and users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you access or use our payment gateway portal, API services, and Android Gateway Application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect necessary information to provide payment routing and automated verification services, including:

  • Account Information: Email address, store name, and authentication details provided during account creation or login.
  • Merchant Configuration: Payment numbers (bKash, Nagad, Rocket), API keys, and Webhook URLs.
  • Transaction & Payment Data: Order ID, payment amount, currency, transaction ID (TrxID), customer phone numbers, and status timestamps required for processing checkout sessions.
  • Android SMS Gateway Data: Payment notification SMS messages received on your connected Android device used exclusively for automated payment verification. Personal or non-payment messages are not processed or stored.

3. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely for operational purposes, such as:

  • Creating and maintaining payment checkout sessions for your store.
  • Automatically matching incoming payment SMS notifications with pending checkout orders.
  • Sending real-time Webhook notifications to your specified website server.
  • Ensuring platform security, authorization, and fraud prevention.

4. Service Availability & Free Plan

NexoraPay is designed to provide accessible automated local payment solutions for merchants. We intend to keep the core service free for as long as possible. In the future, we may introduce optional premium features, advanced analytics, enterprise tier options, or updated plans as the platform evolves.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement standard administrative and technical safeguards to secure your credentials, API keys, and transaction logs. Data is stored securely and retained only for as long as necessary to provide service functionality and audit logs.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time without prior notice. Any modifications will take effect immediately upon being posted on this page. We encourage users to review this page periodically to remain informed of any updates.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us via the NexoraPay Merchant Portal or support channels.
